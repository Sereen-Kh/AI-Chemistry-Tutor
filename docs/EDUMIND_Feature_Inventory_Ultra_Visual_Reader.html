## Readiness Summary

- EduMind already has a strong core: auth, Ask AI chat, RAG retrieval, and flashcard review.
- Most product areas are not fake, but they are still `Partial` because they depend on fallback data, loose contracts, missing frontend tests, or incomplete production workflows.
- The highest-risk user-facing gaps are Study Plan reliability, Quiz submission, Notifications scheduling, Audio media handling, and Dashboard fallback values.
- The clearest future-only areas are Reels/video generation and Subscriptions/billing.
